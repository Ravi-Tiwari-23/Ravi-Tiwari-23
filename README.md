## About Me

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student at **SRM University Delhi-NCR, Sonipat**, and a developer who enjoys taking products from an initial idea to a working system. My practical experience spans full-stack applications, backend services, database integration, authentication, and web deployment.

I have worked on campus printing workflows, property technology, CRM and email automation, e-commerce, satellite-imagery analysis, content systems, and cloud-hosted applications. I care about building software that is useful, secure, maintainable, and clear enough for a team to keep improving.

> **In short:** I build practical products with a backend-first mindset and an eye toward reliable cloud delivery.

## Featured Projects — From Ideas to Working Systems

### 📚 [Bookloom](https://github.com/Ravi-Tiwari-23/Book-formatter) · Document Engineering

**A local, source-faithful PDF-to-book production workspace with approval and quality gates.** Bookloom handles native, scanned, and mixed English/Hindi documents, uses OCR only when required, supports structured editorial review, and produces verified A4 reading editions. Its test suite covers extraction, rendering, integrity, visual QA, and the Streamlit workflow.

`Python` `Streamlit` `PyMuPDF` `Tesseract OCR` `WeasyPrint` `pypdf` `pytest`

### 🏠 [GenVilla / Your Home](https://github.com/Ravi-Tiwari-23/estate_v2) · PropTech

**Property exploration and financial decision support for home buyers.** My work includes product features for property discovery, filtering, location-aware exploration, affordability and true-cost calculations, rent-vs-buy analysis, and government-scheme information. This is collaborative product work; I do not claim sole ownership of the company platform.

`Next.js` `React` `JavaScript` `Tailwind CSS` `React Query` `Recharts` · [Live application](https://your-home-flame.vercel.app)

### 🧑‍💻 [Portfolio & Project CMS](https://github.com/Ravi-Tiwari-23/personal_portfolio) · Full-Stack Application

**A production-oriented portfolio with a focused private administration layer.** It includes project publishing, case studies, profile and résumé management, secure contact storage, media uploads, PostgreSQL support, Cloudinary integration, accessibility work, and application-level security controls.

`Flask` `SQLAlchemy` `PostgreSQL` `Cloudinary` `Gunicorn` `Docker` `pytest`

### ✍️ [Zara It](https://github.com/Ravi-Tiwari-23/ZaraTech-blog_page) · Content Platform

**A role-aware Django publishing application for managing posts, categories, comments, search, and users.** The project combines public reading flows with authentication and a dashboard for editorial and user-management tasks.

`Django` `Python` `Bootstrap` `SQLite` `Pillow`
